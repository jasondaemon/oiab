Canyon Crawler

Blast segmented trail worms before they reach your rover. Destroy cacti, rocks, crates, and cones to clear the wash.

How to Play

Clear every crawler.

Drag in the lower half of the screen or use WASD/arrow keys. The rover auto-fires while moving. Segments split into smaller crawlers when hit.

Spider, flea, and scorpion enemies create pressure in the lower zone. Clear all crawler segments to advance.
